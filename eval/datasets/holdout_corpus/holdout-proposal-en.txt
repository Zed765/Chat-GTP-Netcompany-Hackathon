Proposal: security awareness programme for Foxhollow Foods

Submitted to Sarah Thornbury at Foxhollow Foods GmbH by Greymantle Logistics on 4 February 2026.
Private and confidential.

1. Why now
Two incidents last year at Foxhollow Foods began with an employee opening a convincing email. Neither caused lasting damage, but both took days to contain. Your insurer has since asked for evidence of regular staff training. This proposal describes a twelve-month programme that meets that requirement and, more importantly, changes everyday habits.

2. The programme
Every employee completes a forty-minute introductory course in the first month, in Greek or English. After that we send one simulated phishing email a month, each using a different trick, and anyone who clicks receives a two-minute explanation on the spot. Managers get a short quarterly briefing with the results for their own teams.

3. Workshops for higher-risk teams
Finance, human resources and the executive assistants handle payments and personal records, so they are the people fraudsters target. For these teams we run a half-day workshop in person, built around real examples of invoice fraud and impersonation of senior managers, and we agree a call-back procedure for any change of bank details.

4. How success is measured
We measure the share of staff who click on a simulated email and the share who report it. In comparable organisations the click rate falls from about a quarter to under one in twenty within a year, while reporting rises above half. You receive a dashboard each month and a written review at six and twelve months.

5. Privacy of your staff
The purpose is learning, not discipline. Results are reported by team, never by name, and individual records are visible only to the employee concerned. We keep no copies of mailbox content. Before the programme starts we help you brief the works council and publish a notice explaining what is measured and why.

6. Delivery team and timetable
Laura Pemberton manages the programme and is your contact throughout. Alice Marlowe designs the simulations and leads the workshops. Set-up takes three weeks from 21 February 2026: we import the staff list, agree the first three scenarios with your IT manager and send the announcement. The introductory course opens in week four.

7. Fees
The programme costs €82,000 for the year, for up to two hundred and fifty employees, including the platform, twelve simulations and four workshops. Additional workshops cost €700 each. The fee is invoiced quarterly in advance, payable within 60 days. There is no charge for employees who join during the year.

8. Terms
This proposal remains open for 60 days. The engagement would run under our standard terms of business, a copy of which is attached, together with a data processing agreement covering the staff list. Either side may end the programme at the end of any quarter by giving one month's notice, with no further fees due.
