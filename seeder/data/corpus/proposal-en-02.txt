Statement of Work: data migration for Emberfield Systems

Prepared for: Thomas Whitfield, Emberfield Systems GmbH
Prepared by: Angeliki Anastasiou, Aspida Demo Consulting
Date: 27 March 2026
Reference: AD-2025-233 (project Pharos, PRJ-PHR-2436)

Commercial in confidence. This document is addressed to Emberfield Systems only and must not be passed to third parties.

1. Summary
Emberfield Systems has asked Aspida Demo Consulting to replace the spreadsheets it uses for monthly reporting with a single platform that finance and operations can both trust. We propose a fixed-scope project of 10 weeks, delivered by a team of three, for a total fee of €100,000. The first usable reports would be available at the end of week four.

2. Our understanding of your needs
In our meetings with Thomas Whitfield and the finance team we heard three problems. Month-end closing takes eleven working days because figures are re-keyed by hand. Sales and finance report different revenue numbers for the same month. And nobody can say which version of a spreadsheet is the current one. The project is designed around these three problems and nothing else.

3. Approach
We work in three phases. In discovery we map the existing reports and agree one definition for each figure. In build we load the ledger, billing and order data into a single model and rebuild the twelve reports that matter most. In handover we train the finance team, run one month-end in parallel with the old process and correct any differences we find.

4. Deliverables
You will receive a documented data model, twelve production reports with drill-down to invoice level, a reconciliation between the new reports and the audited figures for the last two quarters, an operations guide written for your own staff, and two half-day training sessions. Source code and configuration are delivered to your repository at the end of each phase.

5. Team
Angeliki Anastasiou is the engagement lead and your single point of contact. Tasos Pavlidis designs the data model and leads the build. A third consultant joins for the build and handover phases. We ask Emberfield Systems to name a product owner with authority to decide definitions, and to give us about four hours a week of a finance analyst's time.

6. Timeline
Work would start on 13 April 2026. Discovery takes two weeks, build takes most of the remaining time and handover the last two weeks, so the project ends around 22 June 2026. The timetable assumes that read access to the ledger and billing systems is in place on day one; every week of delay in access moves the end date by a week.

7. Commercials
The fee for the scope described here is €100,000, excluding VAT, invoiced as 12% on signature and the balance in equal parts at the end of each phase. Payment terms are 30 days. Expenses are not expected; any that arise are charged at cost with your prior approval. Additional reports beyond the twelve are priced at €1,250 each.

8. Risks and how we manage them
The main risk is disagreement on definitions, which is why they are settled in discovery and signed off by your product owner before any report is built. The second is data quality; we report every issue weekly with its owner and due date. The third is key-person dependency on your side, which we reduce by documenting every decision as it is made.

9. Handling of your data
During the project we will see invoices, customer names and payment records. All work is done inside your environment; nothing is copied to our laptops or to third-party tools. Access is limited to the three named consultants and is removed on the last day of the project. Test data is masked before it is used in any demonstration.
