Proposal: reporting platform for Velmont Energy

Prepared for: Emma Keller, Velmont Energy Inc.
Prepared by: Chara Psarra, Aspida Demo Consulting
Date: 23 April 2026
Reference: AD-2026-875 (project Kyma, PRJ-KYM-8255)

Commercial in confidence. This document is addressed to Velmont Energy only and must not be passed to third parties.

1. Summary
Velmont Energy has asked Aspida Demo Consulting to replace the spreadsheets it uses for monthly reporting with a single platform that finance and operations can both trust. We propose a fixed-scope project of 12 weeks, delivered by a team of three, for a total fee of €9,500. The first usable reports would be available at the end of week four.

2. Our understanding of your needs
In our meetings with Emma Keller and the finance team we heard three problems. Month-end closing takes eleven working days because figures are re-keyed by hand. Sales and finance report different revenue numbers for the same month. And nobody can say which version of a spreadsheet is the current one. The project is designed around these three problems and nothing else.

3. Deliverables
You will receive a documented data model, twelve production reports with drill-down to invoice level, a reconciliation between the new reports and the audited figures for the last two quarters, an operations guide written for your own staff, and two half-day training sessions. Source code and configuration are delivered to your repository at the end of each phase.

4. Team
Chara Psarra is the engagement lead and your single point of contact. Evangelia Nikolopoulou designs the data model and leads the build. A third consultant joins for the build and handover phases. We ask Velmont Energy to name a product owner with authority to decide definitions, and to give us about four hours a week of a finance analyst's time.

5. Timeline
Work would start on 10 May 2026. Discovery takes two weeks, build takes most of the remaining time and handover the last two weeks, so the project ends around 2 August 2026. The timetable assumes that read access to the ledger and billing systems is in place on day one; every week of delay in access moves the end date by a week.

6. Commercials
The fee for the scope described here is €9,500, excluding VAT, invoiced as 12% on signature and the balance in equal parts at the end of each phase. Payment terms are 30 days. Expenses are not expected; any that arise are charged at cost with your prior approval. Additional reports beyond the twelve are priced at €800 each.

7. Assumptions
The price assumes one ledger, one billing system and no more than five years of history. It assumes that data quality problems found in discovery are corrected by your team in the source systems, not worked around in the reports. It also assumes that the platform runs in your existing cloud subscription, which you pay for directly.

8. Risks and how we manage them
The main risk is disagreement on definitions, which is why they are settled in discovery and signed off by your product owner before any report is built. The second is data quality; we report every issue weekly with its owner and due date. The third is key-person dependency on your side, which we reduce by documenting every decision as it is made.

9. Handling of your data
During the project we will see invoices, customer names and payment records. All work is done inside your environment; nothing is copied to our laptops or to third-party tools. Access is limited to the three named consultants and is removed on the last day of the project. Test data is masked before it is used in any demonstration.

We will then send the statement of work for signature under the existing agreement and book the discovery workshops. This proposal is valid for 30 days from its date. We would be glad to walk your management team through it at any time.
