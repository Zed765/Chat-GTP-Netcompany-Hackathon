Framework Agreement for Advisory Services with Oakhollow Media

Agreement reference: AD-2024-761

This Agreement is made on 9 February 2026 between Aspida Demo Consulting Single Member S.A., with registered office at 310 Mesogeion Avenue, 155 62 Cholargos, Greece and tax number 650207395 (the "Consultant"), and Oakhollow Media Inc., with registered office at 52 Akti Miaouli Street, 152 32 Chalandri, Greece and tax number 888160619 (the "Client"), represented for the purposes of this Agreement by Claire Aldridge.

The Client wishes to obtain advisory and implementation services, and the Consultant has the skills and staff to provide them. The parties therefore agree as follows.

1. Scope of services
The Consultant shall provide the services described in each statement of work signed under this Agreement, beginning with the work known internally as project Argo (PRJ-ARG-5675). A statement of work sets out the deliverables, the timetable and the team. Work that is not described in a signed statement of work is outside the scope of this Agreement and will not be started without the Client's written request.

2. Term
This Agreement starts on 26 February 2026 and continues for 12 months. It then renews for successive periods of twelve months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current period. Statements of work that are still open when the Agreement ends continue under its terms until they are completed or terminated.

3. Fees and payment
The Client shall pay the Consultant a daily rate of €950 for each consultant day worked, up to a ceiling of €80,000 for the first statement of work. The Consultant invoices at the end of each month and the Client pays within 60 days of the invoice date, by transfer from the account GR7191340818644910535332528. Amounts unpaid after the due date bear interest at 8% a year.

4. Expenses
Travel, accommodation and other out-of-pocket expenses are charged at cost, without mark-up, and only when the Client has approved them in advance in writing. The Consultant keeps receipts for every expense and attaches copies to the invoice. Travel time within Attica is not charged; travel time outside Attica is charged at half the daily rate.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including prices, client lists, source code and business plans. The receiving party may use such information only to perform this Agreement and may disclose it only to staff and advisers who need it and are bound by equivalent duties. These duties last for 3 years after the Agreement ends.

6. Data protection
Where the Consultant processes personal data on the Client's behalf, it acts as processor and follows the Client's documented instructions. The Consultant applies appropriate technical and organisational measures, keeps the data within the European Economic Area, and notifies the Client without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach. Sub-processors need the Client's prior approval.

7. Intellectual property
On full payment, the Client owns the deliverables created specifically for it under a statement of work. The Consultant keeps ownership of its methods, tools, templates and know-how, including anything developed before this Agreement, and grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence to use them as part of the deliverables. Neither party may use the other's name or logo without written consent.

8. Limitation of liability
Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business or indirect loss. The total liability of the Consultant under this Agreement in any twelve-month period is limited to the fees paid by the Client in that period, and in no case exceeds €160,000. Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud, for wilful misconduct or for breach of the confidentiality and data protection clauses.

9. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement or a statement of work by giving 60 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within fifteen days of a written request, or becomes insolvent. On termination the Client pays for the work done up to the termination date and each party returns or destroys the other's confidential information.

10. Change control
Either party may ask for a change to a statement of work. The Consultant replies within five working days with the effect of the change on fees, timetable and team. A change takes effect only when both representatives have signed a change note, and until then the Consultant continues to work to the statement of work as it stands.
