Framework Agreement for Advisory Services with Kestrelby Logistics

Agreement reference: AD-2024-911

This Agreement is made on 11 April 2026 between Aspida Demo Consulting Single Member S.A., with registered office at 310 Mesogeion Avenue, 155 62 Cholargos, Greece and tax number 650207395 (the "Consultant"), and Kestrelby Logistics Ltd, with registered office at 99 Akadimias Street, 382 21 Volos, Greece and tax number 945613733 (the "Client"), represented for the purposes of this Agreement by Emma Moreau.

The Client wishes to obtain advisory and implementation services, and the Consultant has the skills and staff to provide them. The parties therefore agree as follows.

1. Scope of services
The Consultant shall provide the services described in each statement of work signed under this Agreement, beginning with the work known internally as project Tessera (PRJ-TSR-9833). A statement of work sets out the deliverables, the timetable and the team. Work that is not described in a signed statement of work is outside the scope of this Agreement and will not be started without the Client's written request.

2. Term
This Agreement starts on 28 April 2026 and continues for 12 months. It then renews for successive periods of twelve months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the end of the current period. Statements of work that are still open when the Agreement ends continue under its terms until they are completed or terminated.

3. Fees and payment
The Client shall pay the Consultant a daily rate of €900 for each consultant day worked, up to a ceiling of €63,000 for the first statement of work. The Consultant invoices at the end of each month and the Client pays within 60 days of the invoice date, by transfer from the account GR2897034549759214354203270. Amounts unpaid after the due date bear interest at 8% a year.

4. Personnel and governance
Christina Pavlidou leads the engagement for the Consultant and Antonis Kouris is the day-to-day contact. The Client's representative is Emma Moreau, who may approve deliverables and changes on the Client's behalf. The Consultant will not replace a named team member without telling the Client first, and will make sure that any replacement has equivalent experience. The parties meet every month to review progress.

5. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including prices, client lists, source code and business plans. The receiving party may use such information only to perform this Agreement and may disclose it only to staff and advisers who need it and are bound by equivalent duties. These duties last for 5 years after the Agreement ends.

6. Data protection
Where the Consultant processes personal data on the Client's behalf, it acts as processor and follows the Client's documented instructions. The Consultant applies appropriate technical and organisational measures, keeps the data within the European Economic Area, and notifies the Client without undue delay, and in any case within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach. Sub-processors need the Client's prior approval.

7. Limitation of liability
Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business or indirect loss. The total liability of the Consultant under this Agreement in any twelve-month period is limited to the fees paid by the Client in that period, and in no case exceeds €126,000. Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud, for wilful misconduct or for breach of the confidentiality and data protection clauses.

8. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement or a statement of work by giving 60 days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within fifteen days of a written request, or becomes insolvent. On termination the Client pays for the work done up to the termination date and each party returns or destroys the other's confidential information.

9. Change control
Either party may ask for a change to a statement of work. The Consultant replies within five working days with the effect of the change on fees, timetable and team. A change takes effect only when both representatives have signed a change note, and until then the Consultant continues to work to the statement of work as it stands.

10. Governing law and disputes
This Agreement is governed by Greek law. The parties will first try to settle any dispute through a meeting of senior managers held within twenty days of a written request. If that fails, the courts of Athens have exclusive jurisdiction. This Agreement, with its statements of work, is the whole agreement between the parties on its subject and may be amended only in writing.
