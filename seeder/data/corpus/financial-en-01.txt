Account financial summary: Brackenmoor Engineering, Q3 2026

Internal. Finance team only.
Client: Brackenmoor Engineering S.A. (tax number 732006175)
Account owner: Eleni Papadaki
Prepared by: Kostas Gkikas on 6 July 2026
Project: Argo (PRJ-ARG-5675)

Headline figures
Revenue recognised on the account in Q3 2026 was €101,500, against a budget of €15,500. Gross margin was 31%, two points below the firm average, mainly because a senior consultant covered a junior role for five weeks. Year-to-date revenue stands at €19,000, which makes this the firm's fourth largest account.

Billing
Three invoices were issued in the quarter, for a total of €101,500. All three were approved by Charlotte Lindqvist without dispute. Billable days came to 60, of which 30 were delivered on site. The effective daily rate was €700, slightly under the contract rate, because the fixed-price phase overran its estimate by six days that could not be billed.

Receivables
At quarter end the client owed €34,500. Of this, the September invoice is 30 days past its due date. The client's finance department has confirmed that payment was released from account GR0596711306085400078967476 and should reach us within the week. No provision is proposed. Average days sales outstanding on the account over the last four quarters is 60.

Cost of delivery
Staff cost charged to the account was €37,000, including an allocation of bench time. Subcontractor cost was nil. Travel cost of €2,700 was recharged to the client in full under the expenses clause. The overrun on the fixed-price phase cost the firm roughly six consultant days, which the account owner has agreed to absorb rather than raise as a change request.

Risks
The contract renews on 29 October 2026 and the client has asked for a rate reduction of 10% as a condition of renewal. Accepting it in full would take the margin below the firm's floor. The client is also consolidating suppliers, and we understand that two competitors have been invited to present. Concentration risk is moderate: the account is about a twelfth of firm revenue.

Pricing position
Our current daily rate of €700 was agreed two years ago and has not been indexed. The rate card for new clients is about eight per cent higher. The account owner recommends holding the rate, offering a volume discount above 60 days a year instead of a general reduction, and moving the support work to a fixed monthly fee.

Actions
Eleni Papadaki to present the renewal offer to Charlotte Lindqvist before 29 October 2026. Kostas Gkikas to chase the overdue invoice and confirm receipt. Finance to prepare a margin scenario for the discount options by the next account review. Delivery to replace the senior consultant on the junior role by the start of next month, so that margin recovers.

Notes for the partner review
The relationship is in good health: satisfaction in the last survey was nine out of ten, and the client has agreed to act as a reference. The commercial pressure comes from procurement, not from the business sponsor. The firm should decide before the renewal meeting how far below the current rate it is prepared to go, and say no beyond that.

Basis of preparation
Figures are taken from the billing system and the timesheet ledger as closed on 6 July 2026. Revenue is recognised on days delivered for time-and-materials work and on milestones for fixed-price work. Staff cost uses the standard cost rate for each grade. The numbers are unaudited management information and must not be shared outside the finance team.
