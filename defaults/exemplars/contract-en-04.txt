Supply and Services Agreement

THIS AGREEMENT is dated 19 February 2026 and made between Dunbarrow Systems Ltd, whose registered office is at 91 Agias Paraskevis Street, 152 30 Chalandri (the "Supplier"), and Larkspire Media S.A., whose registered office is at Agiou Nikolaou Street 153, 117 49 Athens, Greece (the "Customer"). The Supplier and the Customer are together the "Parties".

1. Supply of goods and services
The Supplier shall sell and the Customer shall buy the Goods ordered under purchase orders accepted by the Supplier. The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using suitably qualified personnel. Each purchase order forms a separate contract on the terms of this Agreement, which prevail over any terms printed on the Customer's order.

2. Price and payment
The price of the Goods and the charges for the Services are set out in Schedule 3 and are exclusive of value added tax. The Supplier may invoice on delivery. The Customer shall pay each invoice in full, without set-off or deduction, within 30 days of its date. Interest accrues daily on overdue sums at 12% per annum until payment.

3. Delivery, risk and title
The Supplier shall deliver the Goods to the address stated in the purchase order. Delivery dates are estimates and time is not of the essence. Risk in the Goods passes to the Customer on delivery. Title passes only when the Supplier has received payment in full for the Goods and for any other sums then due from the Customer.

4. Warranties
The Supplier warrants that for 24 months from delivery the Goods will conform to their specification and be free from material defects in design, material and workmanship. If the Goods do not conform, the Supplier shall, at its option, repair or replace them or refund their price. All other warranties and conditions are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.

5. Confidentiality
Each Party undertakes that it shall not at any time disclose to any person any confidential information concerning the business, affairs, customers or suppliers of the other Party, except to its employees, officers and advisers who need to know it, or as required by law or a competent regulator. This clause survives termination for 5 years.

6. Limitation of liability
Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that, neither Party shall be liable for loss of profit, loss of data or any indirect or consequential loss, and the Supplier's total liability in each contract year shall not exceed €97,000 or the sums paid in that year, whichever is the greater.

7. Term and termination
This Agreement commences on 8 March 2026 and continues for an initial term of 24 months, and thereafter until terminated by either Party on not less than 30 days' written notice. Either Party may terminate with immediate effect if the other commits a material breach which it fails to remedy within thirty days, or enters into insolvency proceedings.

8. General
Neither Party may assign or subcontract its rights or obligations without the prior written consent of the other. A notice must be in writing and delivered by hand, by recorded post or by email to the address notified for that purpose. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties and no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both.

Signed by Hannah Ashdown for and on behalf of the Customer.
