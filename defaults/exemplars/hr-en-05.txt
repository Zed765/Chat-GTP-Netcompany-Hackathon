Personnel file note: performance and compensation review

Human Resources. Strictly confidential: personnel file.
Employee: Marcus Hollis
Position: Senior Account Manager
Line manager: Edward Marlowe
Review period: calendar year 2026
Date of meeting: 13 March 2026

Overall assessment
The employee's overall rating for the year is "exceeds expectations". The line manager notes consistent delivery against sales targets, strong relationships with key customers and a positive influence on newer members of the team. Attendance and punctuality were satisfactory throughout the review period.

Objectives for the year
Four of the five objectives set at the start of the year were met in full. The revenue target was exceeded by 12%. The objective on customer retention was met. The objective to complete the negotiation skills programme was only partly met, because the second module was postponed by the training provider.

Areas for development
Delegation remains an area for development: the employee tends to keep difficult tasks rather than coach others through them. Written reports are sometimes submitted late. The manager and the employee agreed that presenting to senior management more often would build confidence for a future leadership role.

Compensation
Current gross monthly salary is €1,600. Following the review, the manager recommends an increase to €1,700 with effect from 1 January, and a performance bonus of €2,700 payable with the March payroll. The recommendation is subject to approval by the remuneration committee and must not be communicated before then.

Leave and absence
The employee took twenty-two days of annual leave and carries three days forward. There were four days of sickness absence in two separate periods, each supported by a medical certificate. A request for parental leave of two months next spring has been received and will be accommodated; cover is being arranged.

Conduct
There are no disciplinary matters on file for the review period. An informal complaint made by a colleague in the spring, about the tone of an email exchange, was resolved by conversation between the two parties with the manager present. No further action was required and no note was placed on either record.

Training plan
The employee will complete the second module of the negotiation skills programme in the first quarter, attend the company's leadership foundation course in the second, and shadow the regional director for two customer reviews. The training budget allocated for the coming year is €1,100.

Career progression
The employee has expressed interest in a team leader role. The manager considers the employee ready within twelve months, subject to progress on delegation. The employee has been added to the succession plan for the regional sales team. No commitment about timing or grade has been given.
