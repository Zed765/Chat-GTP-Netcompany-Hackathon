Financial performance review, Q1 2026

Pinecroft Capital B.V.
Prepared by the finance department on 4 January 2026. For the board of directors and senior management only.

Summary
Turnover for the quarter was €117,000, 5% higher than in the same quarter last year. Operating profit rose to €92,500. Net profit after tax was €70,000, against a budget of €52,500. The results reflect stronger sales in the domestic market and lower energy costs, partly offset by higher payroll.

Cost of sales and gross margin
Cost of sales increased more slowly than turnover, and gross margin improved to 44%. Raw material prices were lower than budgeted, while freight costs were higher. Inventory write-downs of €2,800 were recorded for slow-moving items. No further write-downs are expected this year.

Operating expenses
Administrative expenses were €50,500, in line with budget. Staff costs grew after the annual pay review and four new hires in sales. Marketing spend was deferred to the next quarter. Depreciation was unchanged. Legal and professional fees included a one-off charge for the refinancing of the term loan.

Working capital
Trade receivables were €50,500 at the end of the quarter, equivalent to 92 days of sales. Two customers account for most of the balance over ninety days, and a provision of €2,800 has been made. Trade payables were paid on average in 30 days. Inventory days fell by four.

Balance sheet
Total assets were broadly unchanged. Net debt fell to €52,500, which is 1.4 times annualised EBITDA. Shareholders' equity increased by the retained profit for the quarter. The company is in compliance with the financial covenants of its loan agreements, with comfortable headroom on both the leverage and the interest cover tests.

Variance against budget
Turnover was ahead of budget by 5% and operating profit by a little more. The favourable variance came from volume and from energy prices. The adverse variances were freight, overtime in the warehouse and the delay in commissioning the new line, which postponed the expected saving in packaging cost.

Outlook
The order book supports the full-year forecast, which management has raised slightly. The main uncertainties are raw material prices and the timing of two large tenders. A price increase of 5% will apply to new orders from next quarter. Capital expenditure for the year is expected to remain within the approved plan.

Taxation and other matters
The effective tax rate for the quarter was twenty-two per cent. The tax audit for the previous two financial years was completed without material findings. The statutory auditors will begin their interim work next month. These management accounts are unaudited and have been prepared on the same basis as the annual financial statements.

Approved for circulation by Alice Marlowe, Chief Financial Officer.
