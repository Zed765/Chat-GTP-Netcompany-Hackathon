Annual performance review

Human Resources. Strictly confidential: personnel file.
Employee: Hannah Renshaw
Position: Senior Account Manager
Line manager: Thomas Thornbury
Review period: calendar year 2026
Date of meeting: 9 September 2026

Objectives for the year
Four of the five objectives set at the start of the year were met in full. The revenue target was exceeded by 5%. The objective on customer retention was met. The objective to complete the negotiation skills programme was only partly met, because the second module was postponed by the training provider.

Strengths
The employee prepares thoroughly, listens well and follows through on commitments made to customers. Colleagues describe a calm and constructive approach under pressure. The employee stepped in to cover a colleague's accounts during a long absence without any drop in service, which the manager wishes to record with thanks.

Compensation
Current gross monthly salary is €1,750. Following the review, the manager recommends an increase to €1,860 with effect from 1 January, and a performance bonus of €2,100 payable with the March payroll. The recommendation is subject to approval by the remuneration committee and must not be communicated before then.

Leave and absence
The employee took twenty-two days of annual leave and carries three days forward. There were four days of sickness absence in two separate periods, each supported by a medical certificate. A request for parental leave of two months next spring has been received and will be accommodated; cover is being arranged.

Conduct
There are no disciplinary matters on file for the review period. An informal complaint made by a colleague in the spring, about the tone of an email exchange, was resolved by conversation between the two parties with the manager present. No further action was required and no note was placed on either record.

Training plan
The employee will complete the second module of the negotiation skills programme in the first quarter, attend the company's leadership foundation course in the second, and shadow the regional director for two customer reviews. The training budget allocated for the coming year is €700.

Career progression
The employee has expressed interest in a team leader role. The manager considers the employee ready within twelve months, subject to progress on delegation. The employee has been added to the succession plan for the regional sales team. No commitment about timing or grade has been given.

Employee comments
The employee agrees with the assessment and thanks the manager for the support received during the year. The employee asks that the workload of the team be reviewed, since two vacancies have remained open for six months, and would welcome clearer criteria for promotion to team leader.
