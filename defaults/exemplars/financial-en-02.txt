Quarterly financial report to the board, Q4 2026

Wintermoor Pharma LLC
Prepared by the finance department on 17 October 2026. For the board of directors and senior management only.

Summary
Turnover for the quarter was €81,000, 8% higher than in the same quarter last year. Operating profit rose to €68,500. Net profit after tax was €104,000, against a budget of €112,000. The results reflect stronger sales in the domestic market and lower energy costs, partly offset by higher payroll.

Revenue
Product sales contributed about two thirds of turnover and services the remainder. The ten largest customers accounted for 31% of revenue. Average selling prices were stable, so the growth came from volume. Export sales fell slightly, because one distributor reduced its stock ahead of a change of warehouse.

Cost of sales and gross margin
Cost of sales increased more slowly than turnover, and gross margin improved to 31%. Raw material prices were lower than budgeted, while freight costs were higher. Inventory write-downs of €2,400 were recorded for slow-moving items. No further write-downs are expected this year.

Operating expenses
Administrative expenses were €33,500, in line with budget. Staff costs grew after the annual pay review and four new hires in sales. Marketing spend was deferred to the next quarter. Depreciation was unchanged. Legal and professional fees included a one-off charge for the refinancing of the term loan.

Cash flow
Cash generated from operations was €68,500. Capital expenditure of €112,000 related mainly to the new packaging line. The company repaid €104,000 of bank debt and paid no dividend. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the quarter stood at €81,000, and the overdraft facility was not used.

Balance sheet
Total assets were broadly unchanged. Net debt fell to €112,000, which is 1.4 times annualised EBITDA. Shareholders' equity increased by the retained profit for the quarter. The company is in compliance with the financial covenants of its loan agreements, with comfortable headroom on both the leverage and the interest cover tests.

Variance against budget
Turnover was ahead of budget by 8% and operating profit by a little more. The favourable variance came from volume and from energy prices. The adverse variances were freight, overtime in the warehouse and the delay in commissioning the new line, which postponed the expected saving in packaging cost.

Taxation and other matters
The effective tax rate for the quarter was twenty-two per cent. The tax audit for the previous two financial years was completed without material findings. The statutory auditors will begin their interim work next month. These management accounts are unaudited and have been prepared on the same basis as the annual financial statements.

Approved for circulation by Thomas Hollis, Chief Financial Officer.
