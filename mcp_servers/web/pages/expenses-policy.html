Travel and expenses policy

This policy explains which business costs the company refunds and how to claim them. It applies to every employee from 1 November 2026.

This version replaces the policy of March 2025. If you still have the earlier version, please ignore it and follow the rules below.

What is refunded

  • Economy flights and second-class rail tickets booked at least seven days ahead.
  • Hotels up to 140 euros a night in Athens and Thessaloniki, and up to 180 euros abroad.
  • Meals up to 35 euros a day while travelling.

How to claim

Submit the claim form with your receipts within 30 days of the trip. Claims above 500 euros need your team lead's approval before the trip.

Refunds are paid with the next payroll run.
