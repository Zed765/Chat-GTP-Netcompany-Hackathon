Setting up the company VPN

Follow these steps to connect your laptop to the company network from home. The setup takes about ten minutes.

  1. Open the software portal and install the VPN client.
  2. Restart your laptop. You must do this before the first connection.
  3. Sign in with your company account and approve the request on your phone.
  4. From now on, connect to the VPN before you open the CRM or the file server.

If you followed the previous instructions from the 2025 guide, remove the old profile first.
